Foundation Load Data for 2.44m Diameter Thickener Bridge

Important Notes:

  • The data provided in this document are the reactions at the supports. Hence, these values shall be multiplied by (-1) to obtain the forces for foundations' design.
  • The local axes shown in the sketches do not correspond to the axes of the pedestals and hence the loads must be re-calculated or transformed to suit to the pedestals' design.
  • The circular section pedestal (for center column) is modelled as a circle of simple supports corresponding tothe circle of anchor bolts.
  • Load cases 604, 606 provides the difference of loads for sanded condition from their respective operating load cases.
  • Hence the same can be combined with 603, 605 respectively to obtain worst case scenarios while formulating the load combinations.
  • Mutual combination of load cases 607 through 615 and their combination with other load cases shall be in accordance with the applicable codes/standards.
  • Special loads such as EQ/WL shall be combined only with the operating condition loads e.g., 603.
  • No differential settlements are considered (both in radial and circumferential directions) in the analysis of the structure. Hence, any differential settlements encountered shall be resolved by considering suitable foundation type.
